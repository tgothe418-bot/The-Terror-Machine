## 3. Positive assertions

### Proposal and acceptance

- Extraction returns an unreviewed source aim, never accepted state.
- General candidate application does not accept the opening aim.
- The explicit acceptance action records exact bytes and real source/evidence provenance.
- Architect correction and proposal do not mutate the draft before explicit Apply.
- Retry re-sends the exact retained User correction after a recoverable failure.

### Identity and opening baseline

- One stable `userCharacterId` survives every boundary.
- The selected cast member, opening-aim target, Engine binding, presence record, and player context all use that exact ID.
- The accepted aim appears once in the actual model prompt as sovereign historical orientation.
- It creates no player pursuit, opportunity, action, internal state, or Autopilot authority.
- A parallel `NONE_DECLARED` fixture reaches the actual prompt with no premise-, inciting-incident-, `cast.goals`-, location-, hard-coded-, or participation-goal substitute.

### Map and placement

- The exact explicit `startingNodeId` survives store, artifact, serialization, normalization, setup, session initialization, and runtime compilation.
- The selected player begins at that exact node.
- A separated NPC is not co-located.
- Offstage and nonlocal cast are absent from local presence.
- Directed exits match authored edges with no synthesized reverse edge.
- The expandable anchor is absent from initial nodes, exits, and presence.
- Source/evidence provenance for map and opening aim resolves to the registered fixture records.

### Non-user intent

- The reviewed NPC pursuit seeds only the existing non-user HG owner.
- The tertiary/quaternary **No readable intent** character has `REVIEWED_NONE` and no fabricated pursuit or independent initiative.
- Descriptive `cast.goals` alone grants no activity eligibility.

### Recovery and containment

- A recoverable Architect failure enables functional retry with exact retained input.
- Terminal source-binding loss does not offer a nonfunctional retry.
- Provider refusal/error data and rejected proposals remain outside draft, Blueprint, Engine context, model prompt, story state, and ordinary exports.
- Retake/refusal preservation at touched Engine boundaries remains unchanged.
